CaseWorthy is a comprehensive case management and social service software platform designed for non-profit and government agencies to collect, manage, and report on client data. It streamlines tracking for services like housing, veterans' affairs, and victim services, allowing organizations to automate workflows, ensure compliance, and measure outcomes using a secure, customizable dashboard

CaseWorthy transforms non-profits, merging program data and operations, and amplifying service providers' impact through technology.
